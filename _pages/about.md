  <h1>About me</h1>
  <p class="academic-home__lead">I study organizations, entrepreneurship, and economic geography—and how they shape careers, inequality, and mental health.</p>
  <p>I am Professor of Strategy and Organization at Aalborg University Business School and Professor II at the Norwegian School of Economics.</p>
  <p>My work connects questions about firms and places with the lives of the people who work in them.</p>
